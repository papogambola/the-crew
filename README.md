# The Crew

A black-ink-on-white-paper game of global crime and deception. One file, no dependencies.

You are the name at the top of the chart. Build a crew from 400+ operatives — each with a
nationality and passport, five attributes, a specialty, an experience rank, a price, and a
secret — then run jobs across 49 cities. Sealed borders need the right passport. Some of
your recruits are informants. Reach 100 notoriety and the final score appears.

## Play it

Open `index.html` in any browser. Progress saves itself in that browser.

To publish it, turn on GitHub Pages for this repository (Settings → Pages → deploy from
the `main` branch, root folder) and it serves at `https://<owner>.github.io/the-crew/`.

## Rules of the world

- **Passports.** Tier 1 is strong, tier 4 is weak. A sealed job needs a crew member who is
  a local national or holds a tier 1–2 passport; without one it is near-impossible.
- **Crew.** Up to 8. Every member takes a cut of each score and costs weekly upkeep.
  Loyalty rises with success and bonuses, falls with failure and missed payroll.
- **Heat.** Every job adds heat; heat penalises every job after it. At 100 there is a raid.
  Laying low for a week cools it.
- **Deception.** About one recruit in fourteen is an informant. Vetting a file costs $6K;
  a dull crew misses one in three. After a big score, low-loyalty members may skim and vanish.
- **Outcomes.** Clean, Success, Messy, Botched, Disaster — from crew power against
  difficulty plus a roll. Botched and worse get people hurt or held.

## Design

Ink on paper: solid-black vector busts, a heavy condensed logotype, monospace ledgers.
Two sexes — male and female — for the player and every operative.
