THE CREW — Game Concept

Working foundation, saved September 14, 2026. This document records the creator's stated ideas. Unspecified mechanics remain open.

## Core concept

THE CREW is a PC game in which the player chooses from a roster of **5,000 characters** to build a **five-person crew, including the player character**: the player plus four other members.

The crew is described as a **global crime interception team**. Clients publish jobs on an in-game dark web, and the player chooses which jobs to accept around the world.

The central challenge is understanding the crew and choosing suitable jobs. Each member's strengths, limitations, history, and role affect the job.

## Character roster and presentation

- There are 5,000 characters to choose from. The number refers to the character roster.
- Characters are male or female. There is no neutral gender option or pronoun system.
- The overall game should be very basic; the intended level of mechanical complexity has not yet been specified.
- Earlier visual direction: a very simple, clean black THE CREW wordmark on a white background, with black-and-white face avatars and changeable characteristics.

## Five-person crew

Every member has an individual profile, covering:

- Attributes and capabilities: what the character can and cannot do.
- Experience.
- Techniques.
- Knowledge.
- Education.
- Time in jail.
- Time on the streets.
- Commander or soldier qualities or role.

The player needs to understand these profiles when selecting the crew and accepting jobs. All relevant aspects of crew members affect the job; specific calculations and outcome rules are not yet defined.

## Countries, categories, and jobs

Jobs vary by country and reflect local context, including:

- Religion and demographics.
- Geography and topography.
- Currency and economic conditions.
- Politics.
- Language and culture.

Each country offers a selection of job categories. Countries can share categories and jobs, but countries do not all have the same categories or the same jobs.

The player can choose the jobs best suited to the crew. Crew abilities and limitations must matter to that choice.
