Get a lost game back

Builds before 98 served the game on a different port every time they started. The browser inside the app files saved games under the address they were saved at — so each launch looked in a new, empty drawer and offered you a new game. Nothing was deleted. The old games are still on your disk, in drawers the app stopped opening.

1 · Close the game

All of it — the window, and any copy still running. The files are locked while it is open.

2 · Gather the files

Press Win+R, type powershell, press Enter, then paste this one line and press Enter. It only reads, copies, and makes a folder on your Desktop.

$d="$env:USERPROFILE\Desktop\crew-save-files"; ni $d -ItemType Directory -Force | Out-Null; gci @("$env:LOCALAPPDATA","$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads","$env:USERPROFILE\Desktop") -Recurse -Force -Filter "*.ldb" -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue; gci @("$env:LOCALAPPDATA","$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads","$env:USERPROFILE\Desktop") -Recurse -Force -Include "*.log","*.ldb" -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue | ? { $_.DirectoryName -like "*Local Storage\leveldb*" } | % { cp $_.FullName (Join-Path $d ("{0}_{1}" -f ($_.Directory.Parent.Parent.Parent.Name), $_.Name)) -Force }; ii $d

A Desktop folder called crew-save-files should open. If it is empty, the game keeps its data somewhere this line did not look — tell me and I will widen it rather than guess.

3 · Drop them here

Select everything in that folder and drag it onto the box. Nothing is uploaded — this page reads the files in your own browser and has nowhere to send them.

4 · Put it back

  1. Open The Crew (build 98 or later — older builds will lose it again).
  2. Press Esc to open the office.
  3. Click the filing cabinet under the desk, on the right.
  4. Paste the code into the box and confirm. The game should name the commander and the week before you commit to it — check it is the one you want.

Recover your best game first and play it a little, so it is saved again under the new fixed address. Importing a second game overwrites the first.
